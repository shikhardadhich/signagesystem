Selfie panel

The right-hand panel with the QR code, the steps and the live thumbnails.

Turn this off for a plain menu board: the panel disappears, the menu fills the whole screen, and selfies stop taking it over. Photos already uploaded are kept — switching it back on brings the wall back exactly as it was.

How it works

Loaded

This board

  • The screen picks up a save on its next poll — no need to touch the TV.
  • More items than fit on a page are dealt out in turn, a page at a time, so a long menu still gets seen.
  • Page turns pause while a selfie has the screen, so nothing cycles past unwatched.
  • A photo that fails to load falls back to the line art, then to the logo, so the board is never blank.
